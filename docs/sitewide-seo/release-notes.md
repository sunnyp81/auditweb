# Site-wide semantic SEO and AI-search revision

AuditWeb's guides mixed overlapping tasks, outdated implementation advice and unsupported service or ranking claims. This revision makes page purpose, evidence, next steps and limitations clearer while preserving the established Astro site and its working resources.

## Content and architecture

- Reviewed 121 page sources generating 180 routes. Edited 113 sources, added the About and methods page, and retained seven page sources after review. Shared metadata, attribution and navigation changes apply across the site.
- Distinguished definitions, hub navigation, DIY procedures, professional scopes, reports and tool comparisons. Added contextual connections between related tasks and the focused SEO service cluster.
- Kept all established page URLs and historical anchor IDs. The 60 city variants describe the same remote service and identify the main service page as canonical. They are excluded from the sitemap; the three noindex utility pages are also excluded. The sitemap contains 117 URLs.
- Added consistent Organization, WebSite and page identities, with Article or other relevant primary content types. Publisher attribution points to visible methods and evidence. City schema follows the shared canonical service identity.
- Corrected retired tools, unsupported thresholds, unsafe pruning or configuration instructions, invented experience and guaranteed-outcome language. Citations point to relevant primary documentation. Update dates describe editorial changes, not new product benchmarks.
- Preserved the working local HTML checker, tool directory, downloads and historical owned-site evidence. Updated privacy wording to the observed form endpoints and added persistent analytics-cookie controls.

## Method and evidence

The semantic and AI-search skills informed task clarity, answer structure, source support, entity consistency and useful linking. Fixed word counts, link quotas, special AI markup and invented semantic scores were not treated as search requirements. Lower-model agents implemented bounded slices; separate reviews and root readback resolved the findings in the review files.

`ownership.json`, the five review ledgers, `coverage.json` and the independent review documents record the source scope and changes. Private search-performance exports and runtime evidence remain outside this public repository.

## Measurement and limits

Search rankings, click growth and AI citations are outcomes to measure after publication. Compare complete 7-day and 28-day periods, separate branded and non-branded queries, and account for country, device and query mix. Monitor Google's selected canonicals for the city variants and record actual AI citations with platform, query and date. Crawler permission is not proof of inclusion.

The existing Terms refund commitment remains an owner-policy question and was preserved. No real enquiry or subscription was submitted during QA; downstream message delivery is outside this content-release validation. Third-party tool comparisons use documentation rather than a new hands-on benchmark.

## Pre-publication validation

- Production build: 180 routes. Rendered HTML validation: zero errors and warnings.
- Route, canonical, schema, metadata, sitemap and internal-target checks: zero findings. All search-facing pages are reachable from the homepage through main-content links; maximum observed depth is three links. No historical anchor IDs were removed.
- Browser sweep: 360 desktop/mobile route checks, 46 automated accessibility checks and zero failures. Representative screenshots and the reopened cookie control were inspected. This does not claim a complete assistive-technology conformance audit.
- Interaction suite: 25 tests passed, including 320px reflow, keyboard navigation, directory filters, checker input safety, downloads, attribution and analytics consent withdrawal.
- Secret scan: no findings in release source, build output or sensitive-file history. Dependency audit: zero reported vulnerabilities.
- Outbound source links: 119 of 120 responded successfully; WebPageTest returned automated-access 403, with its official GitHub documentation available for capability verification.
- Production readback and release identity are recorded in the private release evidence after deployment; these pre-publication checks alone do not establish a live release.
