# Site-wide SEO and AI-search editorial brief

Scope: every existing public route. Preserve Astro, Tailwind, page URLs, working interactions, enquiry destinations, existing files and the established navy/cyan visual system. The reader is a website owner or practitioner choosing an audit method, running a check or acting on evidence. AuditWeb supplies scoped services, a browser-local pasted-HTML checker and downloadable working resources.

## Implementation requirements

- Read every owned source page in full. Use `ownership.json` for exclusive editing boundaries; do not edit other agents' pages, shared layouts, shared data or tests unless separately assigned.
- Give each page a distinct task and direct opening answer, descriptive title/description, logical headings and enough practical detail to execute that task. Preserve useful existing depth; do not replace whole guides with a generic short template. Explain inputs, method, result interpretation and limits where relevant.
- Apply semantic-brief, semantic-audit, content-writer and GEO/AI-search skill principles critically. Plain language, factual evidence and reader usefulness take precedence over rigid comma quotas, blanket early-link bans or mechanically matching competitor headings. Do not invent numeric SEO or AI-readiness scores.
- Make sections understandable when read independently. Use definition, steps, comparison, checklist or decision tables according to the task. Avoid fixed word-count targets, copied boilerplate and repetitive keyword variants.
- Provide primary-source links near substantive technical, legal, product or time-sensitive claims. Browse to verify those claims. Remove unsupported traffic/revenue lifts, audit counts, invented customer outcomes, product ratings and arbitrary SEO thresholds. Never invent first-hand testing, certifications, authors, business locations or client evidence.
- Preserve accurate dates. Add `updateDate="2026-09-11"` to substantively edited ContentLayout pages; this is an editorial update, not a product test or source-check date. Do not change historical review dates.
- Preserve established URL and anchor ownership, TOC targets and useful internal routes. Add a few task-specific parent/sibling/resource links, not site-wide keyword lists. Avoid orphaning previously linked pages. Consolidation by redirect requires a documented reason and root coordination.
- Preserve downloads that exist. Actual resources: 50-check PDF, four blank Markdown toolkit documents and completed owned-site report example. The main web checklist contains 66 checks. There is no universal downloadable Excel spreadsheet, no automatic scoring in those files, and no full remote audit scanner.
- The free checker reads pasted HTML locally. It does not fetch sites, crawl domains, run AI, measure speed/SSL/accessibility or prove Google indexing. Do not rename its controls or alter its implementation.
- Keep service prices consistent with the current main service page: USD 297 / 997 and custom scope. Do not convert USD to GBP by replacing the symbol. Confirm scope rather than promise unverified service features, response times, local offices or certifications.
- Structured data must match visible content. FAQ data may describe actual FAQs but is not a Google rich-result or AI-citation guarantee. No fabricated reviews/ratings, generic Speakable markup or AI visibility multipliers. Root owns shared entity and page schema.
- Google says no special AI schema, llms.txt or writing format is required for its AI features: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide . Search access and training bots are separate. Do not change training policies or promise citations.
- Preserve legal policy commitments. Legal-topic guides must distinguish technical observations from a legal determination, name jurisdiction and link current regulator/standards sources. Security checks must require authorisation for active tests.
- Tables need column headers with scope, a keyboard-focusable horizontal-scroll region when wide, an accessible name and a short overflow cue. Keep one H1 from the shared layout; no new CSS/JS unless required and coordinated.

## Evidence and delivery

Use lower-cost agents for implementation. Do not commit, push, deploy or run concurrent builds. Root reviews the final diff and builds the integrated result. In a separate `docs/sitewide-seo/<owner>-review.json`, record each owned source file, page task, changes (or justified no-change review), primary sources checked and remaining limitations. Record real checks only. Keep private GSC exports and credentials outside this public repository.

The design direction preserves the current evidence-document presentation: clear question headings, legible procedural lists, dated finding records and restrained comparison tables. No decorative redesign is part of this work. Root will validate rendered HTML and internal targets across all routes, run desktop/mobile browser checks and interaction tests, then verify the authorised production release. Search and AI citation outcomes require later measurement and are not release-test results.
