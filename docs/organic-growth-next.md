# Next organic growth experiments

Prepared 5 September 2026. These are prioritised experiments, not traffic forecasts. No external messages, submissions or posts have been sent.

## What the existing evidence says

Saved Search Console window: 6 August–2 September 2026. Site totals: 26,231 impressions and 14 clicks; average position 68.7. This is emerging visibility, not established traffic at scale. The saved page clusters show report examples/guidance at 4,125 impressions and 3 clicks; checklists/templates at 1,028 and 3; tool comparisons at 5,963 and 0. These clusters are not exhaustive and page-level impressions need not sum to property totals. They show a direction to investigate, not independent experiments or keyword volumes.

The strongest immediate bet is making the report/template pages more useful and easier to discover. Broad tool comparisons face a much harder ranking starting point. Saved baseline files remain outside the public repository.

## First 30 days

- Week 1: publish the visual improvements; strengthen the existing report-example page next. Record a single baseline for its queries, clicks and task events.
- Week 2: pilot one noindex tutorial and record the owned-site 404 walkthrough. Do not mass-produce near-identical pages.
- Week 3: prepare five well-matched agency/educator prospects with a specific reason the toolkit would help each. Send only with authorization. Offer optional attribution, not a link exchange.
- Week 4: review actual use and incoming questions. Expand the tutorial or toolkit that people used. Start research only after defining a sample and collecting evidence.

Avoid changing titles repeatedly on very low click counts. Compare at least comparable 28-day windows; segment brand/nonbrand and device. Concurrent design/content changes and low volume prevent strong causal conclusions. Keep a release annotation and distinguish service-link clicks from submitted or qualified enquiries.

## Source checks

- [Google SEO Starter Guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide): supports useful original content, descriptive links and making resources discoverable. The experiment order above is our inference from AuditWeb’s baseline.
- [Google image guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/google-images): supports relevant, descriptive image context and accessible image markup. Visual polish alone is not a ranking promise.
- [Google spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies): constrains scaled low-value pages and manipulative links. Keep resource attribution optional; avoid compulsory keyword-rich badges or reciprocal link arrangements.
- [Ahrefs Website Checker](https://ahrefs.com/website-checker): demonstrates the competing combination of checks, reports and tutorials. AuditWeb needs a narrower useful job and clear scope.
- [SEOFOMO community rules](https://news.seofomo.co/community-rules/): the news community excludes commercial/advertising intent and AI submissions. Do not submit these generated promotional drafts there. It is not a blanket promotion channel.
